Once a idea takes hold, I am consumed by it.
It captures me and requires action.

Sometimes I can alleveate this by writing it down.
Strict detail for a future me.

Other times it just delays the inevitable.

I am driven to create something that does not exist in this universe.
The idea calls to me as if it needs to be birthed.

When I start to work on it, I am in a trance.

I stop eating, stop sleeping, stop talking to people.
I stop feeling anything but my fingertips as I work.

I get lost for hours on end.
Its painful when I am released.


And yet. when I am without it, I feel empty.
