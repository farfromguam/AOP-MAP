Give 100% of your max potential 100% of the time.
Be at your best-best always-always?

Impossible.


Maybe better:

Give 100% of the available, budgeted energy capacity for the task.

Achievable? Maybe.


But that begs the real question:
how much effort do you actually have allocated?


If available is dynamic, and budgeted means consciously allocated from your total reserves, then 100% is not fixed. It moves.

On harder days, on lower-energy days, giving less can still be giving fully.

100% doesn’t always mean max output.
It can mean fully using what you actually, intentionally have to give.
