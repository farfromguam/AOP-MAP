### Structure your time

You get the same amount of time each day.
It is your most precious resource.

Plan for sufficient sleep. Go to bed and wake up at the same time every day. Your life should follow a schedule that works for you.
Allocate specific blocks of time for different activities, including work, sleep, and leisure.

Recognize the natural rhythms and cycles of life, and work with them rather than against them. Use your time to structure your day in a way that aligns with your natural energy levels and habits, your unique needs and preferences.


### Respect your rituals

Allow yourself time to think.
Allow yourself time to prepare.
Allow yourself time to reset.

Whether it's writing in a journal, taking a walk in nature, or practicing yoga, allow yourself to fully immerse in the experience.

As you prepare for your ritual, do so deliberately and with focus. As you engage in your ritual, be present in the moment and give it your full attention. As you complete your ritual, take a few moments to reset and prepare for what's next. Gather your thoughts and avoid becoming burdened by the past or future.

Do not make a habit of skipping the ritual.


### Be present for the time at hand

When it is time to work, do not play.
When it is time to play, do not work.

When it's time to work, Work to the fullest. Give it your undivided attention. Eliminate all distractions and focus solely on the task at hand. Work with intensity, and purpose.

When it's time to play, engage in activities that will truly recharge your energy and spirit. Give yourself permission to fully disconnect.

The boundary between work and play should be clear and non-negotiable. Keep your commitments to your work and your leisure time, and don't allow one to encroach on the other.


### Plan Your Tasks

Define the task at hand.
Understand the requirements.
Ask questions.

To be productive and effective, it's important to define the task at hand and understand what's required and what the end result should look like. Identify any unknowns and work to turn them into knowns before beginning. when this is not possible start with truths and work outwards from there.

Break down your tasks into manageable steps. 
Set specific goals and markers that are required for completion. Allocate time to accomplish the work.

Focus on fully completing each step before moving on to the next, Do not worry too much about the time it will take to complete each step.

Remember that thorough planning is essential to avoiding mistakes, saving time, and achieving better results. With a clear plan in place, you can approach each task with confidence and purpose.


### Meet the Requirements, Exceed Your Standards

Recognize that you are engaging in a contract.
You are agreeing to fully execute the requirements. 
You are agreeing to do it to the best of your ability.    

Set clear standards for the quality of your work and hold yourself to those standards throughout the task.
 
Every task is an opportunity to improve.

Take the time to refine your skills, paying attention to small details and looking for ways to add value.

Remember that by meeting the requirements and exceeding your own standards, you're not only doing good work, but you're also building a reputation for excellence and a strong sense of personal pride.


### Be Thorough

Being thorough is a mindset.
Do not cut corners, do not take shortcuts. 
Do all to the best of your ability.

Being thorough is not just about doing the job well, it's also about maintaining physical and mental readiness. Eat a healthy diet and exercise regularly to keep your body in shape. 

Remember that every task is an opportunity to demonstrate your skill and dedication. You will build a reputation for quality and reliability.


### Respect the Space. Respect the Tools.

Take the time to modify your workspace so that everything has a designated place and is easily accessible. 

In addition, it's important to store tools that won't be used for the current task at hand.

Keep your tools in good condition. Plan for storage including consumables. maintain proactively, Repair when possible, replace when necessary. 



### Keep good notes

Take all the notes. 
Take them all the time.

Digital or physical, keep good notes.
Your notebook is a record of intention and action. 
It holds your past and your plans for the future.

The important thing is to find a system that works for you and stick with it. Take the time to organize your notes in a way that makes sense to you, and make sure that they are available when needed.


Finally, remember that your notes are only as valuable as your ability to reference and review them. Take the time to regularly review your notes, and make sure that they are up to date and accurate.


### Be Curious

Stay curious and engaged in the world around you.
Explore the unknown and discover new possibilities.

Seek out new experiences, ideas, and perspectives. Ask questions and explore topics that interest you, even if they're outside of your comfort zone.

Cultivate a growth mindset, and see every challenge as an opportunity to learn and improve. Don't be afraid to make mistakes, and don't shy away from the unknown.

By embracing curiosity, you can expand your horizons, develop new skills, and gain a deeper understanding of yourself and the world around you. So be bold, be adventurous, and always keep an open mind.


### Share your knowledge

Share your knowledge and expertise with others.

Be generous with your time and insights, and look for opportunities to mentor or teach those around you. Engage in constructive conversations and debates, and be open to new ideas and perspectives.

Recognize that learning is a two-way street - you can learn just as much from others as they can learn from you.
