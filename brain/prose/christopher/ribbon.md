scroll. 

Jack McCauley -- early Oculus, credited with the original scroll wheel (Eric Michelman's Excel prototype at Microsoft is the other half of that story) -- says he meant the wheel to push you into an application. a Z-axis. a third axis of control through 3D or layered content. that's not what happened. it got adopted for vertical scrolling up and down, shipped to the world by Microsoft's IntelliMouse in the mid-90s.

I heard this story and began to question other default interactions and assumptions.
why can't we go left and right.

I have seen exactly one art portfolio website that scrolled left to right. 
and it only kinda worked...

If we can unlock these unused areas then it can be powerful
