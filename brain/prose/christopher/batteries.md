so there I was sitting around thinking about clocks and gears and mainsprings escapement wheels and all of the gubbins that live inside a clock.

the magical mechanisms that slowly and consistently release power over time...Big clocks have gears small clocks have gears. Gears!

Then I looked at my watch and it had a battery. a tiny little cell.
must be some tiny gears in there.

I wanted to *see these tiny gears. they must be different in some way.
And If I am going to look at a new type of gears... Well I should probably look at the gears from a big battery. those gears are probably bigger than the small gears in the small battery...

I am not sure how long this thought sat with me... could be minutes or days or weeks.

---

I needed to see these gears.
So I found the biggest battery I could. 

>>> did you know that a lantern battery 6VD has 4 D-cell batteries in it. -- and a 9 volt battery has 6-AAA batteries in it...  I did not.

Before long I had a D cell in front of me and a hammer in my hand.

My attempts with a screwdriver were unsuccessful.

but together... maybe I could pry the top off... Chisel-like???

The skin was long since gone. The nipple on the top ripped off.
Metal was being pried open... Nothing seemed to budge..

So I started hitting it with a hammer.... 
Thinking If I can't get these gears out cleanly maybe some of them had to be broken...


----

I was close... the metal was beginning to fatigue. for some reason the battery became more rounded... I was hitting it with a hammer after all... so some deformation was expected.

I was locked in.

Thinking if these gears and springs are gonna bounce out... I wanna try and get a good look early/now

I was not going to miss this.
