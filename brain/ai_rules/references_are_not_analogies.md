# References are not analogies

TL;DR: When the user hands you a URL, product name, or example, it is the thing — not an analogy.

#ai_rules #references #framing

-----

If your reading of a reference disagrees with your model of the project, the project model is probably wrong, not the reference.

Origin: the user pointed at rcmap.io and scaletra. I framed them as "analogous community patterns" — when AOP is itself an RC car park and those are its direct peer landscape. The user's correction: "this is for RC cars. that's why I gave you the link. don't assume what you don't know."

How to apply:

- When the user says "look at X," default to "X is the thing, not an analogy."
- If a reference doesn't fit your assumptions about the project, ask before reframing it as tangential.
- Don't soften relevance with "different audience, but the patterns are useful." Either it is relevant directly, or ask why the user picked it.

See `northstar/whats_this_for.md` for the AOP-as-RC-park frame this rule surfaced.
