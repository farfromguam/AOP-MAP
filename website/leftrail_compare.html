Left rail — collapse variations

Two rounds in one page. v1–v5: same locked manilla-tab behavior (unified shell · island cut-out · scoot-up), five visual takes. v6–v10: constraints unlocked, five totally different directions — pill-bar absorption, bottom drawer, workspace switcher, in-place accordion, edge-rail fly-out. Click any control inside a preview to try the behavior.

Round 1 — locked manilla-tab behavior

Five visual takes on a single locked drawer: 44px icon column, fixed row positions, upward/downward absorption, per-card icon toggle.

1 Classic manilla folder

Paper skeuomorphism — die-cut tabs with rounded outer corners, "pulled out" raised state

feelThe icon column reads as the manilla "spine" — slightly darker cream painted continuously behind the tabs, with a 1px paper-grain highlight along the top.

delightClosed tabs sit flush over the spine; open tabs translate left 2px and gain a deeper shadow so they look pulled out of the spine, with a 2px sliver of the darker spine visible behind them.

riskHeaviest treatment — most "physical" feeling, but at the cost of the lightest map-chrome footprint.

2 Modern card stack

Flat, no shadows, state communicated by fill color alone — Linear / Notion sidebar feel

feelZero drop shadows, zero gradients, zero pretend-physical insets. The drawer is a single flat surface bounded by one 1px border.

delightClosed tabs show the cream drawer through. Open tabs fill with moss-soft and pull the glyph to moss-dark. The only ornament: a 2px rust stripe on the active segment's left edge.

riskEasiest to maintain, lightest on the map, but a familiar "tech" look that doesn't pick up the project's hobby texture.

3 Field cabinet (color-coded)

Each tab has a color stripe + 2-letter filing code — provenance fingerprint at the chrome level

feelSearch = moss stripe, Hot = rust stripe, Calendar = brown-dark stripe. Below each glyph sits a tiny SR / HT / CL filing-code label.

delightOpen tab fills with a 12%-alpha wash of its stripe color — opens calendar and the whole tab area glows brown-dark, matching the source-register ink. Reinforces the source-traceable northstar.

riskLeans heavily on hue. Color-blind users have the glyph + 2-letter code as fallbacks, but a high-contrast pass would need patterns (hatch/dot/solid) instead of stripe color.

5 Hybrid — v1 layout + v2 styles

v1's content-aware JS layout engine, v2's flat aesthetic. Active segment carries the rust accent.

feelFlat drawer like v2 (no drop-shadow, no skeuomorphism), but row heights are content-aware like v1 — search row stays tight, calendar's segment grows to its content instead of being clipped to fixed-grid rows.

delightClick an open inactive tab to promote it (rust accent moves) without closing anything. Click the active tab to close it; rust accent migrates to whichever open tab is left.

riskThe "active vs open" distinction may be one click-target subtlety too many for first-time visitors.

4 Dynamic / dimensional

Motion-led — overshoot, hover-shift, segment unfurl, "fingernail past a tab edge" feel

feelIcon column is the spine (never moves). Right segments are cards being pulled out. Open: icon translates 3px on a slight-overshoot spring; soft brown shadow appears beneath the lifted icon.

delightClose adds a 150ms 1deg rotateY wobble — the felt pause of a folder snapping back flush. Hover gives a single-pixel "pull-me" tug. Respects prefers-reduced-motion.

riskMotion takes a position the others don't. If you change your mind about the feel later, it'll be the most expensive variant to undo.

Round 2 — constraints unlocked

Same hard requirements only: pill-bar at top, real DOM IDs preserved, real palette. Everything else (icon column, single drawer, absorption rules, manilla metaphor) is up for grabs. Five different directions to mix attributes from.

6 Pill-bar absorption

Search/hot/calendar become pills in a second row; click → arrow-pointing popover over the map

feelNo vertical card stack at all. Two horizontal pill-rows at the top — original (zoom/preset/3D) above, new (search/hot/cal/×) below. The map owns the whole left strip at rest.

delightPopovers hang from their parent pill with a small downward-pointing arrow. Opening one closes the others; Esc, outside-click, and a dedicated × pill all dismiss.

riskOnly one of search/hot/calendar visible at a time. Calendar (tall) inside a popover means a scrolling body, which may feel less anchored than a fixed left card.

7 Bottom-anchored drawer

Pill-bar stays top-left; search/hot/calendar slide up from the bottom edge in a tabbed drawer

feelMap breathes. At rest, just the pill-bar and three tab pills floating above the bottom edge. Tap a tab, drawer slides up to ~44vh.

delight44px drag handle at the top of the open drawer — drag up to grow, drag down past ~80px to close. Floating rust LIVE chip surfaces above the tab strip when hot is live and the drawer is closed.

riskBottom-edge interaction conflicts on touch with browser chrome / pull-to-refresh. One panel at a time forces tab flips during multi-task work.

8 Workspace switcher

One panel below the pill-bar, top-edge tab strip: Search · Hot · Cal · ∅. One workspace at a time

feelCalmest of the five. The user is only ever doing one thing on the left side, so only one is shown. Panel is content-sized — search is tiny, calendar fills its body.

delightCross-fade swap between workspaces over 140ms. Real ∅ state means "show me only the pill-bar and the map." Pulsing rust pip on the Hot tab when live but the user is on a different workspace.

riskNever see Search and Calendar at once — costs more for a planning-the-event session than for a live-event session. ∅ may be a deceptively important affordance to teach.

9 Stack accordion (no icon column)

Keep the vertical stack. Collapsed cards become 28px peek bars whose text IS the live state.

feelThe shape the user already knows — three stacked cards in a vertical rail. Collapsed = a 28px label bar with live status text: "🔥 Hot now · Stage 3 LIVE", "📅 Next: Sat 2:00 pm Stage 3 — Mud Pit".

delightEven fully collapsed, the user reads the next event and the live hot status without expanding anything. A single "Hide / Show controls" chip above the stack flips all three together.

riskNo icon column means no neutral surface for a rust "active card" accent. About 40px more rail height at minimum than v5's 132px-min footprint.

10 Edge-rail fly-out

36px vertical strip glued to the left edge; rotated-text chips; one shared panel flies out to the right.

feelSmallest idle chrome of any variant — 36px tall strip with three vertical chips. Map essentially unobstructed when nothing is open. Each chip is a permanent spatial anchor.

delightPanel (not chip) is what flies. Clicking another chip cross-fades the inner shell in place. Live pip rides outside-left of the Hot chip so it's visible whether the panel is open or closed.

riskRotated text reads slower than horizontal. One panel at a time. Mobile inversion (rail flips to bottom) doubles the implementation cost.
