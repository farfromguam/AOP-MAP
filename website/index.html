0 POIs, 0 footprints. Pick a type, then Place POI (one click) or Draw footprint (click each corner, press Enter or click the first corner to close). Click a feature to rename or delete it. Edits save in this browser; Export writes aop_editor_pois.geojson. Terrain: AWS Terrain Tiles, derived from USGS 3DEP. Drag with right-click (or two-finger drag) to tilt and rotate. SFWDA raster is © Adventure Off Road Park (via SFWDA) — internal/inspection only.
