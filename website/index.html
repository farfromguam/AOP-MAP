About the Rock Warblers

Friday, June 19, 2026 we host the Trail Blazing Invitational at Adventure Off Road Park — a Rock Warblers crawl on the Tennessee ridge with gated stages, real terrain, and a campfire night run to close it out.

  • FormatThree numbered stages, time-run. Gate touches, rolls, and driver assists cost you. Winching is free.
  • Mandatory skillsWater, a grade pull, and a taco-stand inside every stage.
  • Trail buddiesRequired. Walk, mark, swap. Penalty winch travels with whoever isn’t carrying one.
  • Rigs1/10 electric scale. 1.9″ and 1.55″ wheel classes. No dig, no rear steer. Five scale accessories minimum.
  • Night crawlLights on after dusk. Leaves the pavilion, finishes at the fire.
  • The parkAdventure Off Road Park & Nature Center, South Pittsburg, TN. Six hundred acres of private ridge. Pavilion is base camp: registration, driver meeting, awards, and the fire pit. Camping on-site, first come.
  • The mapOn-day reference built from real terrain data and our own ride evidence. Use it in staging, at the pavilion, and walking a stage. Every line traces back to where it came from.
  • The crewRock Warblers. We build the rigs, walk the stages, throw the days. Find us at the pavilion or out walking a stage.

Built by Rock Warblers, for Rock Warblers. See you at the pavilion.

Satellite tiles served by TDOT/TNMap — inspection use only, not for redistribution. USDA NAIP traces are raw candidates. Keep them out of publish until source, confidence, and field/imagery review are attached. Contours: USGS 3DEP 1 m lidar DEM. Detail tiers by zoom — 50 ft index lines when zoomed out, all 25 ft index lines at mid-zoom, fine 5 ft lines when zoomed in. Cemeteries: TN Comptroller Marion County parcels. Ellis Cemetery is the inholding inside the AOP boundary — the gap in the park polygon. Buildings: FEMA USA Structures footprints over the 9-patch. Raw reference context; verify against imagery before treating as park facilities. Activity hotspots: time-weighted from first-party timestamped GPX. Longer dwell in one cell draws hotter; raw evidence only.
