Contour styling — major vs. minor lines

Each panel draws the real 9-patch lidar contours (gold_aop_contours.geojson, 501 major / 410 minor lines) over the real lidar hillshade. The major (index) lines are identical in every panel; only the minor lines change, so you can pick how faint they should read. All maps are synced — pan or zoom one and the rest follow.

Opacity — fade by transparency Width — fade by going threadlike Hue — fade by blending into the paper
Drag / scroll any map — all panels stay locked to the same view.
Mockup only — not served, not precached. The live layers live in js/viewer_core.js (contours-minor / contours-index and the Topo preset paint overrides). The flat opacity/width below map onto the high-zoom endpoint of those line-opacity / line-width zoom-fades.
