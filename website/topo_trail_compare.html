Topo + trails — fade the topo back, pop the trails

The brown contour topo and the orange trail network compete for attention in the live Topo preset. Each card below renders the real park-core data (contours, trails, water, roads, buildings, forest, hillshade) locked to the same camera, so the colours are judged in context — not on a swatch. The first card is the current shipped look; the rest fade the contours back and lift the trail with brightness + a casing.

Constant in every card: same camera, same data, same base (hillshade / forest / water / roads / buildings). Varied: only the contour colour/opacity/width (index + fine), whether the fine 5 ft lines show at all, and the trail colour/width + casing. Contour fade-by-zoom is preserved in the app; here all schemes show a constant contour density so the colour and weight choices compare directly. Pick a card (or mix attributes) and I'll wire it into the Topo preset paints in js/main.js.

How each card pops the trail: the orange is pushed brighter/more saturated than the flat #f25e0d and given a casing (a wider line drawn underneath) so it separates from the warm browns. How each fades the topo: lower opacity, lighter/neutral hue, thinner lines, or dropping the fine 5 ft tier entirely.
