AOP — Icon master sheet

Every interface icon in website/index.html, collected in one place so they can be compared, normalized, and revised. All share a 22 × 22 canvas; the goal is one stroke width and one optical footprint across the whole set.

Each card shows the icon as built (left, native stroke) beside the normalized version (right, reacting to the controls below). Optical weight = stroke ÷ art extent — it is what the eye actually reads as "thick" or "thin", and it is why icons can share stroke-width:1.6 yet look uneven. Cards whose optical weight or art-fill drifts far from the set median are flagged. Edit any icon's markup inline to revise it, then copy the normalized SVG back into the app.
