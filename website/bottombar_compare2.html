Bottom bar v2 — one edit button, with attribution & info given a home

New direction. Round 1 folded the two stacked bars into a single edit-panel header (label + ⧉ export + ▸ expand) and kept the publish count. This round answers two things that round missed: (1) all you want on the map is a single button that opens the edit panel — so the label and the export button leave the bar entirely (export lives inside the panel once it's open); and (2) the bottom carries more than a count. The map's real attribution is a stack of source credits — TDOT/TNMap & USDA NAIP imagery, USGS 3DEP lidar terrain/contours, USGS NHD water, USGS National Map roads, FEMA/ORNL buildings, TN Comptroller parcels, RiderPlanet visitor context, rendered by MapLibre. Each variant gives the information (publish count) and those attributions one honest home, and reduces the edit panel to one button. (Live count is whatever publish.geojson holds; it is 5 today.)
