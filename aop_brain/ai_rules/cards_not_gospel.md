# Cards are directional

TL;DR: Task cards guide the work. They are not untouchable truth.

#ai_rules #cards #planning

-----

Cards can be drafted with partial information. Research, field evidence, and user corrections can change them.

Use cards for intent and direction. When a card conflicts with a current source fact, a northstar promise, or the user's inline correction, name the conflict and update the card.

Do not silently delete user-written directives. Put analysis below the divider.
