# Fix misspellings

TL;DR: On copy-editing work, fix clear typos directly.

#ai_rules #copy_edit #voice

-----

Do not preserve obvious misspellings on a theory that they might be intentional.

The voice can stay rough, warm, funny, or fragmentary. Spelling still gets cleaned up.

Only flag a word when it is genuinely ambiguous: brand name, dialect, technical term, or content-level uncertainty.
